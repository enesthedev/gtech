# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Car owners.** Mostly on a phone, often arriving from social media. They want their own car calibrated at the workshop. Success: they send the contact form with topic "Workshop".
- **Tuners.** Usually on a desktop. They send ECU/TCU reads and get calibrated files back. Success: they register.

## Product Purpose

GTECH Calibration sells ECU and TCU calibration through two channels:

- **Workshop:** end customers bring their car in.
- **Remote file service:** other tuners send reads and get calibrated files back.

## Positioning

The proof is GTECH's own recorded Dragy runs, stock vs tuned. The claim is measured on the clock, not asserted.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Services (confirmed):** ECU remap (Stage 1/2/3), TCU / gearbox tuning, custom calibration, tuning features (launch control, pops & bangs, speed limiter removal, map switching).
- **Contact:** a single contact form. The file-service CTA goes to Register / Log in.
- **Not built yet:** the file portal behind registration.
- **Out of scope:** payments, multiple languages, a blog, and WhatsApp/phone/Instagram CTAs.

## Brand Commitments

- **Name:** GTECH Calibration (singular; confirmed by the owner on 2026-09-21). Use it in all copy and metadata.
- **Logo master:** `public/gtechcalibrations.png` (dark ink and red, forward slant). It disappears on dark grounds, so dark grounds use `public/brand/gtech-logo-light.webp`. The raster's wordmark reads "CALIBRATIONS"; the SVG master still to come from the owner settles the wordmark.

## Evidence on Hand

- **Dragy runs recorded by GTECH, stock vs tuned.** Metrics: 100–200 km/h, and 1/4 mile (ET plus trap speed). Each run carries the car, stage, mods, a proof link and the run date. Runs are stored in the database and managed by an admin.
- **Absent today:** real runs in the system (sample data only until the admin lands), and GTECH photos.
- **Never invent:** runs, customers, testimonials, prices, turnaround promises or addresses. No stock car photos.

## Product Principles

1. Proof over promise: every performance claim traces to a recorded run with a proof link.
2. Two audiences, two doors: the workshop path and the file-service path stay distinct.
3. Say only what is confirmed; open facts stay open until the owner decides them.

## Open Decisions (owner)

- Whether to show a workshop address.
- A privacy notice under the contact form (the legal text must come from the owner).
- The Register CTA leads tuners into an account with no portal yet. The alternative is to point it at the contact form with topic "File service".
- How homepage success is measured (no analytics is planned).
